# Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Leave Policy is to provide employees with clear guidelines regarding leave entitlements, leave application procedures, and approval requirements while ensuring business continuity and employee well-being.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all permanent, temporary, probationary, and contract employees, unless otherwise specified in their employment agreements or local labor laws.

## 3. Annual Leave

Eligible employees are entitled to annual paid leave as per their employment contract and applicable labor laws. Annual leave should be planned in advance and approved by the employee's reporting manager.

## 4. Sick Leave

Employees who are unable to work due to illness or injury may utilize sick leave. Employees may be required to provide a medical certificate for absences exceeding the period specified by company policy or local regulations.

## 5. Casual Leave

Employees may be granted casual leave for urgent personal matters, family commitments, or unforeseen circumstances. Casual leave should be requested as early as possible and is subject to managerial approval.

## 6. Maternity Leave

Eligible female employees are entitled to maternity leave in accordance with applicable laws and company benefits. Employees should notify the company of their expected leave dates and provide relevant medical documentation.

## 7. Paternity Leave

Eligible employees may be granted paternity leave following the birth or adoption of a child. Leave duration and eligibility will be determined according to company policy and local legal requirements.

## 8. Bereavement Leave

Employees may be granted bereavement leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member. The company may request supporting documentation where appropriate.

## 9. Public Holidays

Employees are entitled to observe recognized public holidays as determined by the company and applicable regulations. Employees required to work on public holidays may receive alternative leave or compensation according to company policy.

## 10. Unpaid Leave

Employees may request unpaid leave when other leave balances have been exhausted or for special circumstances. Approval is subject to business requirements and management discretion.

## 11. Leave Application Procedure

All leave requests should be submitted through the designated leave management system or approved process. Employees are responsible for obtaining approval before commencing planned leave, except in emergency situations.

## 12. Leave Approval Authority

Managers are responsible for reviewing and approving leave requests while considering operational requirements, staffing needs, and employee leave balances.

## 13. Leave During Probation

Employees on probation may be eligible for leave as specified in their employment contract and applicable labor laws. Approval requirements remain the same as for regular employees.

## 14. Leave Records

The Human Resources department will maintain accurate records of employee leave balances, usage, approvals, and related documentation.

## 15. Policy Compliance and Review

Employees must comply with this policy and follow all leave application procedures. The company reserves the right to amend this policy to meet business needs and legal requirements.
