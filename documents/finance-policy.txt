# Finance Policy

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Finance Policy is to establish clear guidelines for managing the organization's financial resources responsibly, ensuring accuracy, transparency, compliance, and accountability in all financial activities.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, managers, contractors, and other personnel involved in financial transactions, budgeting, procurement, expense management, and financial reporting.

## 3. Budget Planning and Approval

Annual budgets shall be prepared by department heads and submitted for review and approval by senior management. Expenditures must remain within approved budget limits unless additional authorization is obtained.

## 4. Financial Authorization

Only authorized personnel may approve financial transactions. Approval limits shall be assigned according to job responsibilities and management levels. Transactions exceeding approved limits require higher-level authorization.

## 5. Expense Management

Employees may claim reimbursement for legitimate business expenses that are properly documented and approved. Original receipts and supporting documents must be submitted within the specified reimbursement period.

## 6. Procurement and Purchasing

All purchases must follow the company's procurement procedures. Employees should obtain appropriate quotations and approvals before committing company funds. Purchasing decisions should prioritize value, quality, and business necessity.

## 7. Cash Handling and Banking

Company funds must be handled securely at all times. Cash receipts should be deposited promptly, and access to cash or bank accounts must be restricted to authorized personnel. Regular reconciliations shall be performed.

## 8. Financial Reporting

Accurate and timely financial records must be maintained in accordance with applicable accounting standards and legal requirements. Monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports shall be prepared and reviewed by management.

## 9. Asset Management

Company assets, including equipment, vehicles, technology, and financial resources, must be used solely for authorized business purposes. Asset inventories shall be maintained and periodically verified.

## 10. Fraud Prevention

The organization maintains a zero-tolerance approach to fraud, theft, corruption, bribery, and financial misconduct. Any suspected fraudulent activity must be reported immediately through designated reporting channels.

## 11. Record Retention

Financial records, invoices, contracts, receipts, tax documents, and supporting materials must be retained for the period required by law and company policy. Records should be stored securely and remain accessible for audits.

## 12. Audits and Compliance

The organization may conduct internal and external audits to verify compliance with financial policies and regulatory requirements. Employees are expected to cooperate fully with audit activities and provide requested information.

## 13. Travel and Entertainment Expenses

Business travel and entertainment expenses must be reasonable, necessary, and directly related to company business. All expenses require supporting documentation and management approval.

## 14. Tax Compliance

The company will comply with all applicable tax laws and regulations. Employees responsible for financial processes must ensure accurate tax calculations, reporting, and timely payments.

## 15. Policy Review and Enforcement

This Finance Policy shall be reviewed periodically to ensure continued effectiveness and compliance with legal requirements. Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, including termination of employment where appropriate.
